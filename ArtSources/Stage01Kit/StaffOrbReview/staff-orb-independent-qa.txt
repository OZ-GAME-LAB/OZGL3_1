No blocking visual defect was identified.

Transparency qualification: the actual material uses the compiled dedicated URP alpha-blend shader, with alpha .40 at a front-facing surface and up to .61 at the edge, ZWrite Off, Cull Back, and one transparent pass. No screen sampling, refraction, extra texture, particle, light, or VFX was added. The current capture has a uniform gray background, so these images alone cannot prove that a contrasting object behind the orb is visible through it. Do not label this capture as a visual transmission test. A separate contrasting-background proof render would verify that presentation explicitly.
